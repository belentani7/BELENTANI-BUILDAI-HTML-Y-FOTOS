About Belentani

An artist and songwriter blending R&B, dark pop and electronic music, creating a world of sonic fantasy.

Born in São Paulo, Brazil, and raised in Barcelona, Belentani represents a fusion of cultures and sounds that transcends traditional genre boundaries. Vocal training at sixteen; first electronic single at twenty-one. When the outer world became too chaotic, he turned his inner sanctuary into a recording studio: pain transmuted into pure aesthetics. No reacciona. Registra. El registro se convierte en canción.
